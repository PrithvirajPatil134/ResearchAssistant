# DBA Thesis Proposal — Organizational Restructuring & GenAI (December 2026)

## Binding Structure (Bordeaux DU Defense)

The DU Research defense document is the primary structural constraint. The thesis proposal must be **25–35 pages** and follow this exact section sequence: [wiki/sources/du-research-defense-procedure, p.1]

- **Page 1**: Header page — institution name (University of Bordeaux + IAE logo), title, subtitle, student full name, student email address, date.
- **Page 2**: Acknowledgments.
- **Page 3**: Abstract and keywords.
- **Page 4**: Table of contents with page numbers.
- **Pages 5 onward**: Main body as follows:

| Section | Page Allocation |
|---------|----------------|
| 1. Introduction | 3–4 pages |
| 2. Review of Literature | 15–18 pages |
| 3. Epistemology and Methodology | 2–3 pages |
| 4. Empirical Study | 2 pages |
| 5. Implications for Management and Business | 1 page |
| 6. Limitations | 1 page |
| 7. Conclusion | 1–2 pages |
| 8. References | No page allocation stated |

[wiki/sources/du-research-defense-procedure, p.1]

The literature review is the dominant section, accounting for 15–18 of the 25–35 total pages. All other body sections combined occupy a maximum of 10 pages. [wiki/sources/du-research-defense-procedure, p.1]

The defense itself lasts one hour total, with a 20-minute candidate presentation using PowerPoint, followed by two rounds of reviewer questions and responses, a thesis advisor segment, and a 5-minute deliberation. [wiki/sources/du-research-defense-procedure, p.2]

The presentation follows a 12–13 slide structure: institution/title/name/date, context of study, interest of topic, research question and sub-questions, two slides on literature review (theories used), epistemology and methodology, data and study, expected outcomes, managerial implication, ethics and data collection, timeline, and references. [wiki/sources/du-research-defense-procedure, p.2]

No score threshold, grading rubric, or DBA Year 2 continuation criteria are stated in the defense procedure document. [wiki/sources/du-research-defense-procedure, p.1–p.2]

No platform (such as Zoom) or specific date range for the defense is stated in the defense procedure document. [wiki/sources/du-research-defense-procedure, p.2]

## Reconciliation: Two Structural Templates

The programme template and the defense procedure overlap significantly but differ on page budget and section granularity.

| Aspect | Programme Template | Bordeaux Defense Procedure | Resolution |
|--------|-------------------|---------------------------|------------|
| Total page range | 20–30 pages | 25–35 pages | Defense procedure applies; 25–35 is the binding constraint for the DU defense |
| Abstract word limit | Max 200 words | Not stated in defense procedure | Programme template applies: 200-word cap |
| Keywords | Up to 12, alphabetically ordered | Not stated in defense procedure | Programme template applies |
| Section count | 8 sections (Abstract through Initial References) | 8 numbered body sections (Introduction through References) | Structurally aligned; defense procedure section titles govern the body |
| Literature review page allocation | "Usually the more extensive part" — no explicit page count | 15–18 pages stated | Defense procedure provides the binding allocation |
| Methodology treatment | One combined Methodology section (§5) | Split into "Epistemology and Methodology" (§3, 2–3 pages) and "Empirical Study" (§4, 2 pages) | Defense procedure splitting governs |
| Research Objectives section | Explicit §3 with three sub-sections (aims, research questions, relevancy) | Subsumed into Introduction per defense procedure section list | Defense procedure does not list a standalone Research Objectives section; aims and research question must be folded into Introduction (§1) |
| Conclusion guidance | "Briefly restate objectives, recap approach, clarify expected findings" | 1–2 pages | Consistent; defence procedure adds the page constraint |

[Sources: wiki/sources/dba-thesis-proposal-template, full document; wiki/sources/du-research-defense-procedure, p.1]

One structural gap: the programme template includes a standalone §3 Research Objectives section (with sub-sections on aims, research questions, and relevancy). The defense procedure's section list does not include this as a separate numbered section. Based on the defense procedure's Introduction description ("context and interest of the topic; objectives of the research proposal; research question; relevance of the study"), these elements must be embedded in Section 1 rather than broken out separately. [wiki/sources/du-research-defense-procedure, p.1; wiki/sources/dba-thesis-proposal-template, §3]

## Current Research Direction

**Topic**: Organizational restructuring triggered by GenAI adoption in large enterprises. [DBA/communication/email_prof_cardoso_progress_update_20260521.md, paragraphs 3–5]

**Research question (revised)**: "How do organizational restructuring patterns differ between technology firms pursuing aggressive generative AI integration and those adopting an incremental approach, and to what extent do these patterns align with the predictions of punctuated equilibrium theory?" [DBA/communication/email_prof_cardoso_response_to_feedback_20260524.md, paragraph on "Narrowing the question"]

**Theoretical lens**: Punctuated equilibrium theory (Tushman and Romanelli, 1985; Romanelli and Tushman, 1994, Academy of Management Journal). The theory predicts that organizations alternate between convergent periods of stability and brief reorientation episodes. Generative AI is framed as the current environmental jolt. [DBA/communication/email_prof_cardoso_progress_update_20260521.md]

**Testable expectation**: Aggressive adopters will show reorientation patterns (concurrent changes to hierarchy, roles, divisions, and headcount within 1–2 quarters); incremental adopters will show convergence patterns (isolated changes spread over longer periods). [DBA/communication/email_prof_cardoso_response_to_feedback_20260524.md, paragraph on "Operationalizing the theory"]

**Method**: Longitudinal archival study using SEC 10-K filings and earnings call transcripts. [DBA/communication/email_prof_cardoso_progress_update_20260521.md; DBA/communication/email_prof_cardoso_response_to_feedback_20260524.md]

**Sample**: 6–8 US-headquartered, publicly listed technology firms with market capitalization above $50 billion. Study period: fiscal years 2021–2027, with 2021–2022 as the pre-transition baseline. [DBA/communication/email_prof_cardoso_response_to_feedback_20260524.md, paragraph on "Empirical boundaries"]

**Operationalization of "structural change"**: Reported changes to headcount, business segments, named leadership roles, and explicitly described organizational design elements in 10-K filings and earnings call transcripts. [DBA/communication/email_prof_cardoso_response_to_feedback_20260524.md]

**Classification of aggressive vs. incremental adopters** (options under consideration, circularity risk acknowledged): (1) AI capital expenditure as proportion of total revenue; (2) CEO strategic framing in public communications; (3) product portfolio pivot; (4) external analyst classification (Gartner, IDC). [DBA/communication/email_prof_cardoso_response_to_feedback_20260524.md]

**Coding framework**: Fixed coding framework derived from established organizational design dimensions (Mintzberg's structural configurations), applied consistently across all firms. Inter-coder reliability checks planned on a subset. [DBA/communication/email_prof_cardoso_response_to_feedback_20260524.md]

**AI-native private firms**: OpenAI and Anthropic acknowledged as central to the phenomenon but currently excluded because SEC disclosures are not available. Potential retrospective incorporation if S-1 filings become available during the study period. [DBA/communication/email_prof_cardoso_response_to_feedback_20260524.md]

## Submission Requirements: AI Citation and Database Access

### AI Citation Policy (APA7)

Universite de Bordeaux requires that AI tool usage be cited in APA7 format. [knowledge/Using Databases/Présentation_DBA_2026_June.pdf, p.91-92]

In-text citation format: `(OpenAI, 2025)` after the relevant passage.

Bibliography entry format:
`OpenAI. (2025). ChatGPT (version 3.5, 19 may) [Large language model]. https://chat.openai.com/chat`

Required components: (1) Entity that created the tool, (2) Year of release used, (3) Software title + specific version, (4) Type descriptor in brackets (e.g., [Large language model]), (5) URL.

Recommendation from the university libraries: include an appendix documenting personal research use of AI, listing: relevant part of the report, AI tool used, instructions/prompts given, date of use, and link to the AI conversation. [knowledge/Using Databases/Présentation_DBA_2026_June.pdf, p.92]

If in doubt, ask the supervising professor. A statement of AI use (or non-use) may be added in the preamble. [knowledge/Using Databases/Présentation_DBA_2026_June.pdf, p.92]
