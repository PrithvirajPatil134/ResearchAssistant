# Literature Review Quality Standards — QNTR Research

## Mandatory Rule: Top Quality Papers Only

When searching for, recommending, or citing research papers for the QNTR literature review:

- **ONLY** focus on papers from top quality ring journals (A*, A, or B rated)
- Ranking systems to use depending on discipline:
  - **Management / Business**: ABDC Journal Quality List (A*, A, B minimum)
  - **Computer Science / IT**: CORE Rankings (A*, A, B minimum)
  - **Cross-disciplinary**: JCR Impact Factor ≥ 2.0 and Q1/Q2 quartile
  - **Law**: Washington & Lee Law Journal Rankings (Top 50)
  - **UK Business**: ABS Academic Journal Guide (3, 4, 4* minimum)
- **Never** include papers from predatory publishers, low-tier journals, blogs, or unranked venues
- Conference papers are acceptable only if the field is nascent and the conference is top-tier (e.g., NeurIPS, ICML, ACM FAccT, AIES)
- Always verify journal ranking before including any paper
- Use peer-reviewed, high-quality sources only — the review should reflect rigor, credibility, and relevance

## Search Strategy Standards

- Follow the Building Block Strategy taught by Prof. Priyanka Suresh
- Document all search keywords, Boolean combinations, and results in the search log Excel
- Use PRISMA framework for systematic reviews
- Synthesize literature, do not merely summarize
