## Summary

Argues that viewing AI agents through principal-agent relationships offers a more actionable perspective than treating them as fully autonomous systems. AI agents are "systems designed to fulfill specific objectives while operating within human-defined constraints" (p. 3). Organizations need governance regimes that integrate agents into organizational processes beyond their technical capabilities.

[Source: data/summaries/literature_review_7papers_analysis.md, Paper 2 §Core Argument]

## Key Arguments

### Distinction of AI Agents

AI agents differ from past AI by "bridging knowledge and action, proactively orchestrating complex workflows" (p. 2).

[Source: data/summaries/literature_review_7papers_analysis.md, Paper 2 §Key Findings]

### Five Guiding Principles for AI Agent Deployment

1. **Adaptability and environmental sensing** (pp. 4-5)
2. **Guided autonomy with defined boundaries of delegation** (p. 5): boundaries "can grow as agents expand their learning"
3. **Multi-agent coordination** resembling multi-disciplinary teams (pp. 5-6)
4. **Combining generative AI with rule-based control mechanisms** for safety and predictability (p. 6)
5. **Alignment/individualization** to stakeholder goals (pp. 6-7)

[Source: data/summaries/literature_review_7papers_analysis.md, Paper 2 §Key Findings]

### Four Principal-Agent Challenges (Table 1, pp. 7-9)

With mitigation strategies:
- Goal misalignment
- Information asymmetry
- Moral crumple zones in responsibility allocation
- Multi-agent complexity

[Source: data/summaries/literature_review_7papers_analysis.md, Paper 2 §Key Findings]

### Organization-Wide Governance

"Organization-wide AI governance strategy is needed to build guardrails for agents" (p. 10). McKinsey example: "a central team reviews all developed agents based on risk, legal, and data policies before rollout" (p. 10).

[Source: data/summaries/literature_review_7papers_analysis.md, Paper 2 §Key Findings]

### Autonomy-Control Tension

Full autonomy is "not always feasible or desirable" (p. 5). Multi-agent complexity: "too many agents with too many tasks can result in overly complex and difficult-to-understand systems" (p. 9).

[Source: data/summaries/literature_review_7papers_analysis.md, Paper 2 §Key Findings]

## Methodology

Conceptual/practitioner-oriented article. Conceptual argumentation supported by illustrative examples from industry (Moderna deploying 3,000 tailored GPTs, p. 9; McKinsey's central review team, p. 10). No empirical data collection reported.

[Source: data/summaries/literature_review_7papers_analysis.md, Paper 2 §Methodology]

## Relevance to Research

- **Theoretical lens (principal-agent theory)**: Direct application of principal-agent theory to AI agents. "The framing of AI agents as 'agents' acting on behalf of human 'principals' (p. 3) maps precisely onto Kolt (2025)."
- **RQ1 (governance practices)**: Five guiding principles and four risk mitigation strategies provide concrete governance practices for agentic AI deployments.
- **P2 (workflow control rigidity)**: "Guided autonomy" with "defined boundaries of delegation" that "can grow as agents expand their learning" (p. 5) directly operationalizes the workflow control rigidity construct.
- **P3 (shadow AI)**: Warning about multi-agent complexity relates to uncontrolled proliferation.
- **P4 (inverted U-shape)**: Explicit tension between autonomy and control suggests an optimal middle ground.

This is "arguably the single most directly relevant paper to the term paper's theoretical framework."

[Source: data/summaries/literature_review_7papers_analysis.md, Paper 2 §Direct Relevance and §Validity]

## Limitations

- Does not provide empirical evidence or quantitative data.
- Does not measure governance outcomes (incident frequency, financial loss).
- Does not test the principal-agent framework with firm-level data.
- Does not address shadow AI directly as a moderating variable.
- Does not propose or test an inverted U-shape relationship.

[Source: data/summaries/literature_review_7papers_analysis.md, Paper 2 §What It Does NOT Do]
