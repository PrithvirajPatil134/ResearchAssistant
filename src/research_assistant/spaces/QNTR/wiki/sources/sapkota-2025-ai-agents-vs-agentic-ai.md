# AI Agents vs. Agentic AI: A Conceptual Taxonomy, Applications and Challenges

## Key Arguments

The abstract distinguishes AI Agents (modular, LLM-driven systems for task-specific automation) from Agentic AI, which it presents as "a paradigm shift marked by multi-agent collaboration, dynamic task decomposition, persistent memory, and coordinated autonomy." It names "hallucination, brittleness, emergent behavior, and coordination failure" as challenges of the agentic paradigm.
