# Cover Email to Prof. Prashar: Candidate Dimensions Draft

**Status**: Draft (user reviews and sends manually)

---

## Email Content

Dear Dr. Prashar,

Here is the first set of candidate dimensions we agreed I would draft Sheth-style and bring to you to challenge. The note is attached. It fixes the constructs before any coding starts, and it keeps Sheth's two artifacts on separate axes: a set of dimensions on which agentic AI is a different governance context, and a table of "From X to Y" shifts those dimensions force.

The short version: four dimensions define the new context, delegated discretion, non-stationarity, inscrutability, and multiplicity, with multiplicity being the piece that makes agentic AI agentic rather than more of the same AI. Speed I have treated as something that runs across all four and sharpens them, not as a fifth dimension. From those four I derive six governance shifts, the Table 1 equivalent.

Your challenge would help me most on four calls I have left genuinely open:

Whether multiplicity is one dimension or two. It currently carries both agents interacting with other agents and agents spawning sub-agents. Parsimony says keep them together; splitting is cheap now and expensive later.

Whether speed should be promoted to a fifth dimension instead of an intensifier. I lean toward intensifier, but the argument for promoting it is real.

Whether six governance domains are the right cut. The coverage test holds for the six I chose, but the cut is a judgment, not a derivation.

And the honest soft spot: primary field study of governance inside deployed agentic systems is still thin, so the frame leans partly on adjacent evidence from rule-based automation. I have flagged where that extrapolation does work and where it stretches. I would value your read on whether that is acceptable for the frame or whether it needs more agentic-specific field evidence before we build on it.

Tell me which dimensions hold and which do not. Push wherever it looks soft. Whenever you have a chance is fine.

Warm regards,
Prithviraj
