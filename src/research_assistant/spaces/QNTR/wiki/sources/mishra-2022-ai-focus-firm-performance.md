# Artificial intelligence focus and firm performance

## Key Arguments

The abstract examines "the link between firms' focus on AI in their 10-K reports and their gross and net operating efficiency," and reports that "AI focus is associated with improvements in net profitability, net operating efficiency and return on marketing-related investment while reducing adspend and creating jobs."

[Abstract-only; full text required — no effect sizes are taken from the abstract]

## Methodology

Abstract describes an empirical study using "a system of simultaneous equations" to test the relationship between AI focus (measured from 10-K reports) and operating efficiency for US-listed firms.
