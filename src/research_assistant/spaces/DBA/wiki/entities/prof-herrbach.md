## Role

Instructor for Course 6, "Human Resources and Organizational Behavior" (UE1, 20 Doctoral Credits Points), in the IAE Bordeaux DBA 2026 programme. Course topics include talent management, motivation theories (Maslow, Herzberg, Deci & Ryan), employee engagement, leadership theories (transformational, transactional, servant leadership), organizational culture, diversity and inclusion, theories of organizational change (Lewin, Kotter), work-life balance, well-being, and technology in HR including HR analytics and AI in HR.
