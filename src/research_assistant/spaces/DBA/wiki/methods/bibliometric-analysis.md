# Bibliometric Analysis (Bibliometrix + VOSviewer)

A quantitative and qualitative approach to mapping academic literature, taught in the DBA programme (Management of Business Models, Entrepreneurship and Innovation course) and required for the course assignment.

## When to Use

When studying the performance of academic literature linked to a research object and when justifying article selection for a literature review. The course assignment requires "the process to choose the article thanks to a bibliographic analysis cf. bibliometrix and VOS Viewer." [DBA_BM_ENT_INN_N2_04June2026.pdf, p.3]

## Tools

Two complementary tools are taught:

1. **Bibliometrix** (R language via RStudio): "When you need to study the performance of the academic literature linked to your object of study (quantitative approach of the analysis)." [DBA_BM_ENT_INN_N2_04June2026.pdf, p.36]

2. **VOSviewer** (Java): "When you need to identify collaboration networks between researchers and major trends in key words + Qualitative approach = analysis by yourself thanks to VOS Viewer." [DBA_BM_ENT_INN_N2_04June2026.pdf, p.36]

## Data Source

The workflow starts with exporting bibliographic data from Scopus (or Web of Science) via university library access (Bordeaux ENT). [DBA_BM_ENT_INN_N2_04June2026.pdf, p.37]

Key steps as presented in the deck:
- Write keywords using Boolean operators (AND for combining concepts, OR for synonyms; compound words in inverted commas). [DBA_BM_ENT_INN_N2_04June2026.pdf, p.38]
- Refine scope: add year range, filter by field (Business Management and Accounting, Economics, Econometrics and Finance). [DBA_BM_ENT_INN_N2_04June2026.pdf, p.38]
- Limit results to 50–70 references representing the core of the subject. [DBA_BM_ENT_INN_N2_04June2026.pdf, p.38]
- Export as CSV with all 5 items (citation information, bibliographical information, abstract, etc.). [DBA_BM_ENT_INN_N2_04June2026.pdf, p.38]

## Bibliometrix Capabilities (as presented)

- Main information overview [DBA_BM_ENT_INN_N2_04June2026.pdf, p.41]
- Annual scientific production [DBA_BM_ENT_INN_N2_04June2026.pdf, p.41]
- Most relevant sources (journals) [DBA_BM_ENT_INN_N2_04June2026.pdf, p.41]
- Most frequent author keywords [DBA_BM_ENT_INN_N2_04June2026.pdf, p.41]
- Word clouds [DBA_BM_ENT_INN_N2_04June2026.pdf, p.41]
- Country collaboration world map [DBA_BM_ENT_INN_N2_04June2026.pdf, p.41]

Invocation command: `bibliometrix::biblioshiny()` in R console. [DBA_BM_ENT_INN_N2_04June2026.pdf, p.39]

## VOSviewer Capabilities (as presented)

Three analysis types from the deck:
- **Bibliographic coupling** (analysis of the Present): identifies main current articles on a subject. [DBA_BM_ENT_INN_N2_04June2026.pdf, p.42]
- **Co-citation** (analysis of the Past): identifies main theoretical foundations. [DBA_BM_ENT_INN_N2_04June2026.pdf, p.42]
- **Co-occurrence** (author keywords): maps keyword networks. [DBA_BM_ENT_INN_N2_04June2026.pdf, p.42]

Interpretation rules from the deck:
- Larger node size = subject more explored by researchers. [DBA_BM_ENT_INN_N2_04June2026.pdf, p.42]
- Smaller node size = subject less explored. [DBA_BM_ENT_INN_N2_04June2026.pdf, p.42]
- Greater distance between keywords = co-occurrence less explored. [DBA_BM_ENT_INN_N2_04June2026.pdf, p.42]
