# Procedure for Certificate of Research (DU) Defenses

## Thesis Proposal Structure

The thesis must be between 25 and 35 pages. [Procedure for Certificate of Research (DU) Defenses.pdf, p.1]

The required page and section layout is as follows:

- **Page 1**: Header page — institution name (University of Bordeaux + IAE logo), title, subtitle, student full name, student email address, date. [p.1]
- **Page 2**: Acknowledgments. [p.1]
- **Page 3**: Abstract and keywords. [p.1]
- **Page 4**: Table of contents (with page numbers). [p.1]
- **Pages 5 and following**: Main body sections as below. [p.1]

Main body sections with stated page allocations:

1. **Introduction** (3–4 pages): context and interest of the topic; objectives of the research proposal; research question (problematic); relevance of the study — what the researcher wants to do. [p.1]
2. **Review of Literature** (15–18 pages). [p.1]
3. **Epistemology and Methodology** (2–3 pages): model, hypothesis, statistics, qualitative approach, quantitative approach, or mixed — what is planned. [p.1]
4. **Empirical Study** (2 pages): proposition, data, field of study. [p.1]
5. **Implications for Management and Business** (1 page). [p.1]
6. **Limitations** (1 page). [p.1]
7. **Conclusion** (1–2 pages). [p.1]
8. **References** (no page allocation stated). [p.1]

## Defense Format and Timing

Total estimated duration: 1 hour. [Procedure for Certificate of Research (DU) Defenses.pdf, p.2]

The candidate presents their thesis using a PowerPoint presentation. The minute-by-minute breakdown is: [p.2]

| Segment | Duration |
|---------|----------|
| Candidate's presentation | 20 minutes |
| Reviewer 1 questions | 10 minutes |
| Candidate's response to Reviewer 1 | 5 minutes |
| Reviewer 2 questions | 10 minutes |
| Candidate's response to Reviewer 2 | 5 minutes |
| Thesis advisor | 5 minutes |
| Deliberation | 5 minutes |

No platform (e.g., Zoom) or specific date range is stated in the document. [p.2]

## Presentation Slide Template

The document specifies a 12–13 slide presentation. The required slide content is: [Procedure for Certificate of Research (DU) Defenses.pdf, p.2]

- **Slide 1**: Institution, title of the research, name, date.
- **Slide 2**: Context of the study.
- **Slide 3**: Interest of the topic.
- **Slide 4**: Research question (problematic) and sub-questions of research.
- **Slide 5**: Review of literature — what theories are being used?
- **Slide 6**: Review of literature — what theories are being used? (continued)
- **Slide 7**: Epistemology and methodology.
- **Slide 8**: Data and study.
- **Slide 9**: What are the expected outcomes?
- **Slide 10**: Managerial implication.
- **Slide 11**: Ethics — how to collect data and conduct interviews.
- **Slide 12**: Timeline.
- **Slide 13**: References.

## Relevance to Research

This document defines the binding structural requirements for the DU Research defense. The section structure and page allocations here supersede the generic programme template wherever they conflict. The user must submit a 25–35 page proposal following the header page, acknowledgments, abstract, TOC, and numbered section sequence specified above. The literature review is the dominant section (15–18 of the 25–35 pages), followed by introduction (3–4 pages) and epistemology/methodology (2–3 pages). All other sections are 1–2 pages each.
