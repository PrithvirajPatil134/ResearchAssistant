**What it is**

The Academic Journal Guide (AJG) published by the Chartered Association of Business Schools (Chartered ABS). There is no local file for this list; it lives online only. The portal describes the guide as supporting academics and Deans "in the navigation of the research environment and its complexity, through the provision of robust evidence informed information and data," with ratings "based upon peer review, editorial and expert judgements" [charteredabs.org/academic-journal-guide/, fetched 2026-07-28]. The most recent edition is 2024, described on the portal as "an intermediate review incorporating new journals as well as journals where there have been significant shifts in metrics" [same].

**What it is for**

The journal-quality gate for UK Business sources in QNTR literature search. `.kiro/steering/literature-review-standards.md` names the "ABS Academic Journal Guide (3, 4, 4* minimum)" as the UK Business standard [literature-review-standards.md, §"Ranking systems to use"]. Use the AJG to confirm a business journal's ABS rating before including a paper.

**How to use**

Open `https://charteredabs.org/academic-journal-guide/`, follow the "View the Academic Journal Guide 2024" link, and log in or register (access is gated). The interactive table lets you search, sort, and filter journals to read a title's rating. Under the QNTR standard, include the paper only if the journal is rated 3, 4, or 4*.

**Caveat**: The full rating scale and the searchable table are behind a login on the portal; the exact tier labels were not visible on the public landing page as fetched, so the 3 / 4 / 4* threshold above is stated per the steering file, not read off the portal [charteredabs.org/academic-journal-guide/2024, fetched 2026-07-28 — table gated]. Rating methodology is documented in a separate methodology PDF linked from the guide page. This is a UK-business list; it overlaps with but is distinct from the ABDC list (see `abdc-jql-2022-journal-ranking.md`).
