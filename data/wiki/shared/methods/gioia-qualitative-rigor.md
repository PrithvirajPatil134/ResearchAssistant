# Gioia Method

## What It Is

A systematic approach to inductive qualitative research that produces credible, publishable findings. 3,600+ citations. Published in Organizational Research Methods (top methods journal).

## When to Use

- Building new concepts or theory from qualitative data (interviews, documents, observations)
- Need to demonstrate rigor to reviewers who are skeptical of qualitative work
- Want structured coding that produces a visual "data structure" diagram

## The Three-Step Process

1. **First-order codes**: Use informants' own words/terms. Stay close to the data. At this stage you are a "glorified reporter." Example: "We flattened the org," "player-coaches replaced managers."

2. **Second-order themes**: Researcher-created groupings of first-order codes into theoretically meaningful categories. Example: grouping "flattened the org" + "eliminated VP layer" + "reduced management spans" into a theme called "hierarchical compression."

3. **Aggregate dimensions**: Higher-level abstractions that connect themes to theory. Example: "hierarchical compression" + "role hybridization" + "function consolidation" roll up to an aggregate dimension "structural simplification."

## Key Output: Data Structure Diagram

A visual showing the progression from raw data (left) through themes (middle) to dimensions (right). This diagram IS the core deliverable of Gioia-method research. Reviewers evaluate your work largely through this visual.

## Ground Assumptions

- Organizational reality is socially constructed
- Informants are "knowledgeable agents" who can explain their own experience
- Researcher avoids imposing prior constructs in early coding stages
- Existing theoretical categories are held in abeyance until second-order coding

## Relevance to DBA Thesis (Org Restructuring)

If the thesis uses qualitative content analysis of public documents (10-K filings, CEO memos, earnings calls), the Gioia method provides the coding structure:
- First-order: the actual language companies use about restructuring
- Second-order: researcher groupings of those descriptions into structural change themes
- Aggregate: the organizational archetypes or transformation patterns that emerge

## Key Difference from Generic Thematic Analysis

Gioia explicitly separates informant language (first-order) from researcher interpretation (second-order). This dual structure is what gives it rigor. Generic thematic analysis (Braun & Clarke 2006) does not enforce this separation.
