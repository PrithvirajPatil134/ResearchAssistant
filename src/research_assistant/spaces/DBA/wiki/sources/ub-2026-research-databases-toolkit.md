## Documentary Research Methodology

### Multi-Step Approach

The toolkit defines the aim of literature research as "to take stock of scientific knowledge on a given subject and to document the evolution of this knowledge." [Toolkit_DBA_2026_Academic_Research.pdf, p.1]

The methodology follows four steps: [Toolkit_DBA_2026_Academic_Research.pdf, p.1]

1. Identify the concepts
2. Translate them into keywords
3. Expand your search with synonyms
4. Write your research equation

### Boolean Operators and Search Tips

Three Boolean operators are defined: [Toolkit_DBA_2026_Academic_Research.pdf, p.3; Présentation_DBA_2026_June.pdf, p.32–34]

- **OR**: all documents containing one or both search terms
- **AND**: all documents containing both search terms simultaneously
- **NOT / SAUF**: all documents containing the first term but not the second (use sparingly)

Additional search syntax:
- **Truncation** (wildcard): `account*` → accounting, account(s), etc.
- **Quotation marks**: `"corporate social responsibility"` → exact phrase search
- **Parentheses/Brackets**: structure query and control search term order

### Concept Grid

A concept grid template is provided where one concept equals one column, with synonyms listed below each concept and linked by OR, while concepts are connected by AND. [Toolkit_DBA_2026_Academic_Research.pdf, p.4–5]

### Document Watch Table

A tracking table is recommended with columns: Database, Search equation, Date, Results, Technical feedback, Reflexive feedback. [Toolkit_DBA_2026_Academic_Research.pdf, p.6–7]

### Translation Requirement

Keywords must be translated into English because "most research is published in English" and "non-English articles are described in English in the databases." Deepl and Linguee are recommended as translation tools. [Présentation_DBA_2026_June.pdf, p.28–29]

## UB-Accessible Databases

The following databases are confirmed accessible through the Université de Bordeaux student account (Aquipass card + Intranet login): [Toolkit_DBA_2026_Academic_Research.pdf, p.2; Présentation_DBA_2026_June.pdf, pp.13–14, 51, 64–86]

| Database | Coverage |
|----------|----------|
| **Babord+** | UB's unified library catalog and search engine (books, journals, theses, e-resources) |
| **Scopus** | Multidisciplinary (30% humanities, 27% experimental science, 26% health, 17% life science); supports guided and advanced search with Boolean operators; citation analysis, author profiles, affiliation search |
| **EBSCO Business Source Premier** | Leading database for economics and management; includes EconLit with Full Text, Regional Business News, Vente & Gestion |
| **CAIRN** | French-language social science journals and e-books |
| **HAL** (Hyper Articles en Ligne) | Open-access French research deposit platform; durability of data, quality of reporting |
| **ISIDORE** | Humanities and social sciences search engine (3000+ digital data sources) |
| **OpenEdition** | French-language scientific journals, books (PDF/EPUB free; HTML freemium), and research blogs; sections in Business & Economics |
| **Theses.fr** | All French doctoral theses (defended or in progress) |

[Présentation_DBA_2026_June.pdf, pp.64–84; Toolkit_DBA_2026_Academic_Research.pdf, p.2]

### Google Scholar Library Link

Setup: Settings > Library links > enter "Bordeaux" > select "Université de Bordeaux." This displays "Accès Univ. Bordeaux" links in search results, providing access to documents via the UB portal. [Toolkit_DBA_2026_Academic_Research.pdf, p.8; Présentation_DBA_2026_June.pdf, p.41]

### Search Paths Hierarchy

The presentation distinguishes four search paths with different characteristics: [Présentation_DBA_2026_June.pdf, p.38]

1. **Babord+**: quick, guided access to scientific information
2. **Databases subscribed by the university**: wide variety but not always clear or specific
3. **Search engines (Google Scholar)**: quick route but requires double-checking
4. **Artificial intelligence**: sometimes incorrect or too general; suggestions need verification

## AI Citation Policy (APA7)

Université de Bordeaux requires that AI tool usage be cited in APA7 format. [Présentation_DBA_2026_June.pdf, p.91–92]

### In-Text Citation

Quote the relevant passage in quotation marks, then include a citation at the end: `(OpenAI, 2025)`. [p.91]

### Bibliography Entry Format

Required components: [p.91]
1. Entity that created the tool (e.g., OpenAI, Mistral)
2. Year of the release used
3. Software title + specific version used (e.g., ChatGPT 3.5 May 19 version)
4. Type descriptor in brackets (e.g., [Large language model])
5. URL

Example: `OpenAI. (2025). ChatGPT (version 3.5, 19 may) [Large language model]. https://chat.openai.com/chat` [p.91]

### AI Use Appendix (Recommended)

Recommendation from the libraries: include an appendix documenting personal research use of AI, with columns: [p.92]
- Relevant part of the report
- Artificial intelligence tool used
- Instructions for use (prompts given)
- Date of use
- Link to the AI conversation

A statement of AI use (or non-use) may be added in the preamble. If in doubt, ask the supervising professor. [p.92]

## Plagiarism Standards

UB's Charter to combat plagiarism applies, based on Article L122-4 of the French Intellectual Property Code: "Any representation or reproduction in whole or in part without the consent of the author [...] is unlawful." [Présentation_DBA_2026_June.pdf, p.88; Toolkit_DBA_2026_Academic_Research.pdf, p.9]

### Most Common Forms of Plagiarism

- Copying and pasting without citing the source
- Paraphrasing without citing the source
- Self-plagiarism: reusing one's own previously written work without attribution
- Distributing work purchased from a third party
- Translating a document without citing the source

[Présentation_DBA_2026_June.pdf, p.88; Toolkit_DBA_2026_Academic_Research.pdf, p.9]

### How to Avoid Plagiarism

Three steps: [Toolkit_DBA_2026_Academic_Research.pdf, p.9]

1. Choose and respect a citation standard (e.g., APA7)
2. Make the citation in the text — direct quotes require quotation marks, author/title citation, and must be short relative to the original text
3. Enter the reference in the bibliography

[Toolkit_DBA_2026_Academic_Research.pdf, p.9; Présentation_DBA_2026_June.pdf, p.88–89]
