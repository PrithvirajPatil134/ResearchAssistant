# Algorithmic decision-making and system destructiveness: A case of automatic debt recovery

## Key Arguments

The abstract states that information systems literature has "not sufficiently examined the destructive effects of governmental ADM" and reports a case study of the Australian government's "Robodebt" programme, which "ended up causing severe distress to citizens and welfare agency staff." Drawing on systems thinking and organisational limits, it develops a model explaining "how a socially destructive government ADM programme was initiated, sustained, and delegitimized," and emphasises "the importance of setting up robust governance infrastructures for ADM programmes."
