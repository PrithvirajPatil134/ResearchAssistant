**What it is**

A QNTR Session 1-2 supplementary workbook demonstrating measures of variability on a worked dataset. Unlike the other three artifacts, this is not a journal-quality gate; it is a statistics teaching aid.

**What it is for**

A worked reference for computing and interpreting variability statistics: range, standard deviation, z-scores, and distribution/tail concepts. Useful as a refresher when a QNTR deliverable involves descriptive statistics.

**Structure** (from `--meta` + cell peek)

Six sheets, each building on one worked example [S1-2 QNTR 2025 Supp Material - Variability.xlsx, --meta]:
- `Range` (26 rows x 2 cols) — the base example: Mean = 27.7, Median = 28, Range = 35 [sheet 'Range', rows 1-3].
- `St Dev` (26 x 4) — standard-deviation computation: sum of squared deviations from the mean = 1076.2, n - 1 = 19, sample standard deviation sx = 7.526 [sheet 'St Dev', rows 1-3].
- `Z-score` (26 x 10) — carries the same mean/SD summary and extends to z-score columns [sheet 'Z-score', rows 1-3].
- `Distribution` (119 x 10), `Right tailed` (119 x 10), `Left tailed` (119 x 10) — larger worked tables illustrating distribution shape and one-tailed (right / left) regions, each repeating the mean/SD summary block [sheets, rows 1-3].

**How to use**

Open the sheet matching the concept you need (range, SD, z-score, or tail region) and follow the worked cells. The n = 20 example (n - 1 = 19) with mean 27.7 and sx = 7.526 is the anchor carried across all sheets [sheet 'St Dev', rows 1-3].
