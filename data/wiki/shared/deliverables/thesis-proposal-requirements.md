# Thesis Proposal Structure Requirements

Consolidated from Prof. Cardoso (DBA Programme, Bordeaux) and Prof. Sandeep (CRO Course, IIM Sambalpur).

## Cardoso's Mandatory Structure (Projet de These)

The DBA thesis proposal must follow this exact structure. 25-30 pages strict limit. French professors grade only pages 1-30.

| Section | Page Allocation |
|---|---|
| Title | — |
| Abstract | — |
| Introduction (why relevant? significance? contribution?) | 2-12 pages |
| Literature Review (who studied this? gaps? theoretical framework?) | 2-5 pages |
| Objective and Research Questions | — |
| Methods (design, data collection, sampling, analysis) | 1-4 pages |
| Planning / Research Timeline | — |
| Results and Conclusion (expected outcomes, limitations, implications) | — |
| References | — |

## Cardoso's Objective Construction Rules

- Start with: "The aim of this research is to..."
- Pick a verb: analyze, assess, compare, determine, examine, evaluate, explore, identify, investigate, propose
- Pick a complement: the impact of, the effect(s) of, the performance of, the implications of, the relationship of/between, the factors, the adoption, the preferences, the drivers, the practices/a framework, the reason(s)
- Specify what, where, when, who

## Cardoso's Title Construction Rules

- Format: General statement + ":" or "?" + Specific focus (who, where, when, how, why)
- Using a question mark increases reader interest
- Title and objective must complement each other and be coherent
- Have multiple title options; narrow down based on objective phrasing

## Cardoso's Causal Language Hierarchy

Be careful about language implying causation beyond what the design supports:
- Correlation < Influence < Impact < Moderation
- "How" in an objective already assumes a relationship exists; "if" is more neutral
- Any assumptions must be supported by the literature review

## Cardoso's Key Constraints

- 30-page limit is STRICT
- Strictly follow the structure provided
- Relevance section: max 3-5 sentences, general to specific, should read like a story
- By November/December 2025 (Year 1), expected to produce thesis proposal
- The proposal becomes the main structure of the thesis paper

## Sandeep's Quality Principles (Complementary)

These apply on top of Cardoso's structure:

**Research Question Criteria (must pass all):**
- Clear and focused?
- Clearly showing dependent and independent variables?
- Challenging but answerable?
- Are needed data and literature available?
- Can you do analytical analysis, not just descriptive?

**Gordon Davis Rule:** Use visuals (figures/tables) to show models, results, and robustness checks. Reviewers read visually. Show all hypotheses in a model figure.

**Methodology Options (valid for this programme):**
- Qualitative using case study / interviews
- Survey
- Laboratory experiment
- Field experiments
- Data from firms/industry
- Textual analysis
- Secondary data (collected by others and publicly available)

**Expected Contribution Section Must Answer:**
- What are the expected results?
- What are their implications?
- What insights can managers learn?
- How does it contribute to the conversation in the discipline (theoretical contribution)?
- What are the limitations?

**Precision, Rigour, Clarity:**
- Parsimonious with words, precise with meanings
- Adhere closely to specified format, word limits, headings, fonts, referencing
- Clear, persuasive communication that addresses qualms
