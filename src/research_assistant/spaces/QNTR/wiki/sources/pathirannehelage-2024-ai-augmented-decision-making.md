# Design principles for artificial intelligence-augmented decision making: An action design research study

## Key Arguments

The abstract contrasts traditional decision support systems (DSS), which rely on "fixed decision rules and models" and "human agentic primacy," with AI systems that "learn, adapt, and act autonomously, demanding recognition of IS agency." It argues that extrapolating prescriptive design knowledge from conventional DSS to AI-augmented decision making is "problematic" given the shift in "autonomy, responsibility, and accountability" and "the corresponding risks of stochastic outputs," and calls for novel design principles.

## Methodology

Abstract describes an action design research (ADR) study within an e-commerce clothing company, building an AI-augmented decision-making system for marketing, consumer engagement, and product design.
