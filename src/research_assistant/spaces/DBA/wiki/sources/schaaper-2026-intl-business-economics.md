## Schaaper's Research Focus

Based on the course materials, Schaaper's published research centers on:

- **HQ-subsidiary control mechanisms**: formalization (reporting, written documents), centralization of decisions, socialization (visits, informal coordination, corporate culture), and expatriate deployment as control levers
- **Quantitative methods**: uses structural equation modeling to test relationships between control dimensions and subsidiary characteristics (ownership structure, production activities, geographic distance)
- **Geographic focus**: French MNCs in Asia (China, Japan, Southeast Asia); also Chinese firms in France (reverse FDI)
- **Collaborators**: Bruno Amann (Toulouse 3), Jacques Jaussaud (Pau), Ni Gao (Kedge Bordeaux)

[Source: slides 2, 5, 6, 7 title pages and content]

## Key Concepts from Materials

- **Four control mechanisms for MNC subsidiaries**: centralization, formalization, socialization, expatriate deployment
- **Compensatory control**: when a MNC holds a low capital share, it compensates via increased formalization and training/socialization
- **Flexpatriates**: international business travelers + short-term assignees, used as alternative to traditional long-term expatriates
- **Regional Management Centers (RMCs)**: intermediate coordination layer between HQ and subsidiaries; located in Singapore (South Asia clusters), Hong Kong/Shanghai (North Asia clusters)
