## Email Content

Dear Dr. Prashar,

Following up on our plan from June, here is the first set of candidate dimensions, built Sheth-style, for you to challenge. I have put them in a Google Doc so we can iterate the way we discussed:

[Google Doc link, commenter access]

The note keeps Sheth's two artifacts on separate axes: four dimensions on which agentic AI is a different governance context (delegated discretion, non-stationarity, inscrutability, and multiplicity), and a table of "From / To" governance shifts those dimensions force. Multiplicity is the piece I am proposing as the increment that makes agentic AI distinct rather than more of the same AI. Speed I have treated as something that runs across all four and sharpens them, not as a fifth dimension.

Comments directly in the Doc would help me most, wherever a dimension looks soft. Four calls are where your judgment matters most, and I have flagged each as an open question at the end of the note:

- whether multiplicity is one dimension or two (it currently carries both agent-to-agent interaction and sub-agent spawning);
- whether speed deserves promotion to a fifth dimension rather than an intensifier;
- whether six governance domains are the right cut;
- and the honest soft spot, that field evidence inside deployed agentic systems is still thin, so the frame leans partly on adjacent automation evidence.

Once you have had a chance to comment and we agree on which dimensions hold, my thinking on the path forward is to use the settled frame as the coding scheme for the review corpus and build the gap table you suggested in May (Han et al. style) on top of it. I will develop that into a fuller draft for your review, and we can set up a call to talk it through at that stage. For now, your comments on the dimensions and a quick word on whether that sequence works are all I need.

Warm regards,
Prithviraj
