# Agentic Governance Research — Refactored Approach (v2)

## Why the Refactor

The v1 approach (in the WIP doc) jumped to model-building before conducting a thorough literature survey. We designed constructs (Orchestration Maturity, Workflow Determinism, Liability Gap) and hypotheses without first mapping what the existing literature already covers and where the actual gaps are.

A top-tier journal won't publish a paper that merely "proves a hypothesis with data." The contribution must be theoretical — a new mechanism, a new explanation, or a new lens on how agentic AI governance impacts business that wasn't understood before. We can only identify that contribution after a proper literature survey.

## The Corrected Sequence

1. **Conduct thorough literature review** — map the landscape of what's been studied
2. **Extract limitations & future research** — from each relevant paper, identify what the authors themselves say is missing
3. **Identify the specific gap** — where existing research stops and ours begins
4. **Build the model around the gap** — not the other way around
5. **Design measurement** — constructs and items that are feasible to collect (single-respondent, firm-level)

## Iterative Paper Discovery Methodology

Rather than searching hundreds of papers upfront, we use an iterative approach:

### Round 1 (Current — 5 papers)
- Start with the 5 high-quality papers already identified
- Extract: constructs studied, methodology, key findings, limitations, future research directions
- From limitations/future research sections, identify new search terms and cited papers worth following

### Round 2 (Next — 10-15 papers)
- Follow citation trails from Round 1 (backward: who did they cite? forward: who cited them?)
- Use new search terms discovered in Round 1 limitations
- Screen for ABDC A*/A/B, CORE A*/A/B, JCR Q1/Q2 only
- Extract same fields

### Round 3 (Refinement — 20-30 papers total)
- Fill remaining thematic gaps
- Build concept map showing which relationships are studied vs. unstudied
- Identify THE gap for our paper

### Round 4 (Saturation)
- Confirm no major papers are missing
- Finalize the gap statement
- Build the model

## Quality Gate (Steering Rule)
Per workspace steering rule: ONLY top quality ring papers from high-impact journals.
- Management/Business: ABDC A*, A, B
- Computer Science/IT: CORE A*, A, B
- Cross-disciplinary: JCR Impact Factor ≥ 2.0, Q1/Q2
- Law: Washington & Lee Top 50
- Conference papers: Only if field is nascent AND venue is top-tier (NeurIPS, ICML, FAccT, AIES)
