# Operationalising AI governance through ethics-based auditing: an industry case study

## Key Arguments

The abstract defines ethics-based auditing (EBA) as "a structured process whereby an entity's past or present behaviour is assessed for consistency with moral principles or norms" and reports that the main difficulties large multinational organisations face in EBA "mirror classical governance challenges," including "ensuring harmonised standards across decentralised organisations, demarcating the scope of the audit, driving internal communication and change management, and measuring actual outcomes."

## Methodology

Abstract describes a 12-month longitudinal industry case study observing AstraZeneca (a biopharmaceutical company) as it prepared for and underwent an ethics-based AI audit.
