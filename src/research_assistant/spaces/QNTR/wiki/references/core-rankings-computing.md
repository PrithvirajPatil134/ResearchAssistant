**What it is**

The CORE Rankings portal for Computing venues. There is no local file for this list; it lives online only. The portal now presents itself as the "ICORE Conference Portal," noting that "CORE rankings is now an international collaboration named ICORE" [portal.core.edu.au/conf-ranks/, fetched 2026-07-28]. Search is by Title, Acronym, Rank, or Field of Research, against an edition selector spanning ICORE2026, CORE2023, CORE2021, and older editions back to CORE2008.

**What it is for**

The journal/conference-quality gate for Computer Science / IT sources in QNTR literature search. `.kiro/steering/literature-review-standards.md` names "CORE Rankings (A*, A, B minimum)" as the Computer Science / IT standard [literature-review-standards.md, §"Ranking systems to use"]. Use the portal to confirm a computing venue's CORE tier before including a paper.

**Rating scale** (as observed on the portal, ICORE2026 summary)

Tiers: A*, A, B, Australasian B, C, Australasian C, and Other [portal.core.edu.au/conf-ranks/, fetched 2026-07-28]. Under the QNTR standard, only A*, A, and B clear the gate.

**How to use**

Open `https://portal.core.edu.au/conf-ranks/`, search by venue title or acronym, select the edition closest to the paper's year, and read the assigned rank. Include the paper only if the venue is A*, A, or B per the standard.

**Caveat**: The active portal ranks conferences, which is the relevant scope for computing where top conferences (e.g. NeurIPS, ICML) are primary venues. A separate CORE *journal* ranking existed but "has been discontinued as of February 2022 in order to focus on conference rankings," is "no longer supported or updated," and was last refreshed in 2020 [portal.core.edu.au/jnl-ranks/, fetched 2026-07-28]. For computing *journals*, fall back to the JCR cross-disciplinary gate (see `jcri-impact-factors-2025.md`).
